# corpus-map packs withdrawn (2026-09-02)

`corpus-map-500-2026-07-08/` and `corpus-map-pilot-2026-07-06/` are no longer
distributed in this tree. Their source corpus was personal — including private
correspondence with third parties — and personal data does not belong in a
public evidence pack, however useful the run was. The methods those runs
exercised (corpus mapping over entity lists) are unaffected; no published
number in README/PROGRAM cites these packs. The runs remain archived
privately by the operator.
