## 2. STRONGEST OBJECTION TO THE CURRENT TRAJECTORY

The hazardous state is not "too many findings"; it is unbounded operator-side WIP with no feedback signal. Nothing tells the operator (a) which proposed-to-operator items are still open, (b) which pre-registered 30-day tests (dated 2026-08-08, sitting only inside notes/red-team-2026-07-09/doctrine.md with no owner or schedule) are due, or (c) that a standing external commitment — PUBLIC_BENCH's "we commit to same-week runs on every frontier release" — exists with no watch mechanism, so a missed release stales the public surface silently. Every additional instrument, wave, or panel adds to the claim surface that this one controller must audit, while adding zero audit capacity. Under the current structure, unacceptable-loss (2) — meaning drowning in churn — is not a risk; it is the steady-state output of the design. The 07-09 standing finding ("the validation loop closes through one person; distribution is the unlisted binding constraint") already named this; it stands, and P2 is the one partial exception.
